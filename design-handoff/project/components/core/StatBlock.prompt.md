Big editorial statistic — a Inter-900 figure (optionally with a small unit) plus a label. The signature way fei presents impact numbers.

```jsx
<StatBlock value="257" unit="kWp" label="Installiert" sub="4 Projekte" accent="green" />
<StatBlock value="380" unit="+" label="Mitglieder" accent="white" />
```

Accents: `green` (default — display figure on light), `yellow` (on green/navy), `navy`, `white` (on dark). Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Format numbers German-style (`1.500`, `4 %`).
