Renders one of the 21 bundled animated brand outline icons (navy stroke + yellow accent GIFs). Raster — cannot be recoloured; keep on cream/white, never on yellow.

```jsx
<BrandIcon name="solar-panel" size={64} />
<BrandIcon name="growing-plant" framed alt="Wachstum" />
```

Set `base` to point at your copy of `assets/icons` (default `assets/icons`). Names: solar-panel, electric-car, bar-chart-growth, dividends, home, location-pin, approve-checked, clock-time, consultation, growing-plant, recycling, water-drop, cloud, magnifier-search, bicycle, person-protest, privacy-policy, rules, glasses-eye, desert, melting-glacier.
